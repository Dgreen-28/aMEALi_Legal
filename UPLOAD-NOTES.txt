STUDIO DG APPS — WEBSITE
========================
Drop-in replacement for Dgreen-28/Dgreen-28.github.io

HOW TO UPLOAD
-------------
1. github.com/Dgreen-28/Dgreen-28.github.io
2. "Add file" -> "Upload files"
3. Drag in: index.html, support.html, privacy.html, shop.html,
   styles.css, and the whole assets/ folder
4. Commit. Pages redeploys in about a minute.

app-ads.txt and README.md are untouched — leave them as they are.

THE VOICE
---------
Rewritten in first person, plain and warm. The headline is
"I make apps, and I really hope you enjoy them." The about section says
you have a regular job and make apps in the hours around it because you
enjoy it, and that you're glad when someone tells you they had fun with
one. Change any of it freely — it's all plain text near the top of
index.html.

Your name "Corey" appears twice (hero paragraph, about section). If you'd
rather stay just "Studio DG", delete those two sentences and nothing else
breaks.

THE LOOK
--------
- Light Carolina blue instead of the dark version. Hero sits on a soft
  blue wash that fades into near-white.
- The background lines are still there, front and centre: the same grid
  and construction circles that sit behind your DG mark.
- Carolina blue #4B9CD3 leads, emerald #0E9F6E for the small category
  labels, your navy #131E43 for headings and the logo.
- Poppins for headings — it's the closest match to your wordmark's
  letterforms, so the type and the logo belong together.
- No mono "technical" labels this time. Those were what made it read
  like a developer built it.
