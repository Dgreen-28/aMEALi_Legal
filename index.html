About

A bit about me.

I'm Corey. I have a regular job, and I make apps in the hours around it because I genuinely enjoy it — the designing, the building, the fiddling with a detail nobody else will notice.

Everything you see here I did myself: the code, the icons, the App Store screenshots, and the replies when you email support. That means things ship slowly. It also means if you tell me something's broken, you're telling the person who can fix it.

I'm not trying to build the next big thing. I just want to make apps that are pleasant to use, and I'm always glad when someone tells me they enjoyed one.
